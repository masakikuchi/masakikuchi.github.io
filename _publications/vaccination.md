date: 2023-03-17
venue: 'BMC Public Health'
paperurl: 'https://bmcpublichealth.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12889-023-15376-6'
summary: >-
  This article investigates why COVID-19 vaccination progressed unevenly across Japan in 2021. Regression analyses at the prefectural and municipal levels find a positive association between governing-coalition representation and vaccination progress, even after accounting for local infection and demographic conditions. The results highlight how electoral incentives can influence public-health implementation.
