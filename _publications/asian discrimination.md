title: "How Asians React to Discrimination Does Not Depend on Their Party Identification"
collection: publications
category: manuscripts
status: published
permalink: /publication/Asian discrimination
date: 2021-10-04
venue: 'Socius'
paperurl: 'https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/23780231211048023'
summary: >-
  Using a national quota-based survey of 2,482 Asians in the United States and automatic text analysis of respondents’ reflections on anti-Asian discrimination during the COVID-19 pandemic, this study finds that reactions were broadly similar across partisan identities in both substance and tone. The results identify a limit to partisan polarization in perceptions of group-based discrimination.
