title: "How Does War Affect Cultural Tolerance? Evidence from Concert Programs, 1900--1960"

venue: 'Journal of Peace Research (Leading Article)'
paperurl: 'https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00223433221123362'
summary: >-
  This article examines how major wars reshape tolerance toward foreign culture using original records from 29,135 concerts and 125,530 pieces performed by ten symphony orchestras across five countries from 1900 to 1960. It finds that performances of music from enemy countries declined sharply during wartime, while postwar recovery was faster in defeated countries than in victorious ones—suggesting that security concerns and relative power shape cultural exchange.
