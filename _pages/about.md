Welcome! I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the [Department of Political Science](https://polisci.wustl.edu/) at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://wustl.edu/). I was previously a Research Fellow for [Japan Society for the Promotion of Science](https://www.jsps.go.jp/english/e-pd/index.html).

My research examines how inter-state conflict and political violence shape domestic politics. Across my projects, I study how social identities structure the domestic effects of war, foreign threats, and political violence on public opinion, inter-group relations, and social cohesion. In the dissertation, I explore how unequal wartime burdens among subnational groups in times of inter-state war affect state-society and inter-group relations. Methodologically, I use observational data, survey experiments, and computational methods. My work has been published or is forthcoming in peer-reviewed journals, including *Journal of Peace Research* and *BMC Public Health*.


I received my BA (2019) and MA (2021) in Political Science from [Waseda University](https://www.waseda.jp/top/en/), Japan. Outside of academic work, I enjoy playing the violin and cheering for the Cardinals.
