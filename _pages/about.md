Welcome! I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the [Department of Political Science](https://polisci.wustl.edu/) at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://wustl.edu/). I was previously a Research Fellow for [Japan Society for the Promotion of Science](https://www.jsps.go.jp/english/e-pd/index.html).

My research examines how inter-state conflict and political violence shape domestic politics, with a particular focus on how social identities structure their effects on public opinion, inter-group relations, and social cohesion. My dissertation advances this agenda by studying how unequal wartime burdens among subnational groups affect state-society relations and inter-group cohesion. Methodologically, I use observational data, survey experiments, and computational methods. My work has been published or is forthcoming in peer-reviewed journals, including *Journal of Peace Research* and *BMC Public Health*.


I received my BA (2019) and MA (2021) in Political Science from [Waseda University](https://www.waseda.jp/top/en/), Japan. Outside of academic work, I enjoy playing the violin and cheering for the Cardinals.
