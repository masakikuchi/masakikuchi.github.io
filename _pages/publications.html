Research

Research Interests

I study the mass politics of international security, asking how war and political violence shape public opinion, state–society relations, and intergroup relations. My dissertation examines how the unequal distribution of wartime burdens across social groups and regions affects social cohesion, and how group identities condition those effects. Methodologically, I combine observational causal inference, survey experiments, and computational text analysis.
